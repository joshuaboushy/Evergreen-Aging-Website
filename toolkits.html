Safety Audit

12-Point Home Fall-Proofing Environmental Safety Audit

Over 60% of senior falls occur within the home. This systematic room-by-room audit identifies throw rugs, lighting hazards, and stair issues before an accident occurs.

Public Health Standard: Public Health Agency of Canada (PHAC) Reference [1] →
Daily Mobility Routine

"Morning Gentle Joint Warm-Up" Printable Routine

A large-print, 5-minute seated routine seniors can do right beside the bed to circulate synovial fluid before walking: ankle pumps, seated knee extensions, gentle pelvic tilts, and shoulder rolls. Directly combats morning stiffness and fear of movement.

Public Health Standard: Mayo Clinic Health System Reference [6] →
