Evergreen's Quality Standards

When you are caring for an aging loved one, the last thing you need is misinformation. I follow a strict, science-backed sourcing standard. Every routine, myth-buster, and printable safety toolkit I share is grounded directly in peer-reviewed clinical studies and verified public health guidelines. I work hard to translate complex geriatric research into practical habits you can use at home today.
