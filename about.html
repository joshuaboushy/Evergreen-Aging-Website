Why Evergreen Aging Exists: Radical Accessibility for Senior Health

Too often, older adults are told that chronic pain, weakness, and loss of independence are inevitable consequences of growing older. I founded Evergreen Aging to help shatter that myth with compassionate, accessible, and evidence-based education.

Four Core Commitments

1

Radical Accessibility

Designed specifically for older eyes and hands: scalable high-contrast typography, dedicated low-vision viewing modes, and full screen-reader compatibility.

2

Ending Preventable Falls

Falls result in over 3 million emergency department visits each year. By sharing clinical balance assessments (CDC STEADI) and simple home safety audits, I work to empower families to prevent injuries before they happen.

3

Translating Geriatric Science

Cutting-edge medical journals contain important information about sarcopenia, bone remodeling, and dementia prevention. I translate dense clinical research into clear, practical daily habits anyone can adopt.

4

Supporting the Care Circle

Millions of adult children, spouses, and personal support workers assist older adults every day. I provide respectful, dignity-first communication tools and home safety sheets to support sustainable caregiving.

Collaborative Health Literacy

Even the best doctor appointments have a lot of important medical ground to cover in a short visit. Because your physician needs to focus on immediate health concerns, complex conditions, and medications, there usually is not enough time during an appointment to practice balance exercises together, practice safe ways to stand up from a chair, or review tripping hazards around the house.

Evergreen Aging works right alongside your family doctor, geriatrician, occupational and physical therapists, and personal support workers to continue that care at home. I take trusted medical advice and turn it into simple, printable checklists to make your living space safer, easy movement habits that help keep you steady on your feet, and clear guidance that older adults, family care partners, and frontline workers can put into practice every day.

My goal is to give you and your team everyday tools to stay strong, confident, and independent at home.

Clinical Sourcing & Verification Standards

To protect senior health and counter health misinformation on the internet, I follow a strict Zero-Speculation Sourcing Standard. I do not promote unverified supplements, fad diets, or non-peer-reviewed wellness trends.

🇨🇦

Health Canada, PHAC & CSEP

Canadian 24-Hour Movement Guidelines (CSEP 65+), PHAC Seniors' Falls in Canada surveillance, Osteoporosis Canada spine-sparing protocols, and healthy aging nutrition.

🏥

Mayo Clinic Health System

Clinical guidelines on joint preservation in osteoarthritis, progressive strength for sarcopenia, senior hydration physiology, and ambulatory balance therapy.

🛡️

CDC STEADI Initiative

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention's Stopping Elderly Accidents, Deaths, & Injuries clinical algorithm for primary care.

🔬

National Institute on Aging (NIH)

Evidence-based longevity science regarding Alzheimer's/dementia risk factors, sleep architecture, and caregiver resilience.

Meet the Founder: Joshua Boushy

The personal passion and clinical motivation behind Evergreen Aging.

✉️ Email Joshua ▶ YouTube Channel

Joshua Boushy

Founder, Evergreen Aging
🎓 B.Sc. Human Kinetics • University of Guelph

I’m a Human Kinetics student at the University of Guelph, and I spend a lot of my time thinking about how our bodies change as we age: balance, muscle, and how to keep the nervous system sharp for the long haul.

I’ve volunteered in long-term care homes for years, and noticed one pattern repeat itself. When someone becomes afraid of falling, they start moving less... and the less they move, the faster they lose strength and confidence. It can happen quickly. And in a 15-minute doctor’s appointment, there’s rarely time to walk someone through safe balance or point out the hazards in their own home.

That’s why I started Evergreen Aging: a completely free resource with printable checklists, caregiver phrasing that keeps a person’s dignity front and centre, and simple follow-along routines you can do without ever getting down on the floor. My goal is to take what the research says and make it feel doable.

When I’m not studying or working on Evergreen, you’ll find me at the gym, hiking, camping, or fishing, ideally with friends and family nearby.
