Why Evergreen Aging Exists: Radical Accessibility for Senior Health

Too often, older adults are told that chronic pain, weakness, and loss of independence are inevitable consequences of growing older. As a public health initiative, Evergreen Aging exists to shatter that myth with compassionate, accessible, and evidence-based education.

Our 4 Public Service Commitments

1

Radical Accessibility

No tiny unreadable text, no confusing fitness apps, no paywalls. Every lesson, routine, and safety guide is free, printable, and formatted with large, high-contrast typography and read-aloud options.

2

Ending Preventable Falls

Falls result in over 3 million emergency department visits each year. By spreading clinical balance assessments (CDC STEADI) and simple home safety audits, we empower families to prevent injuries before they happen.

3

Translating Geriatric Science

Cutting-edge medical journals contain powerful revelations regarding sarcopenia, bone remodeling, and dementia prevention. We translate dense clinical research into clear, practical daily habits anyone can adopt.

4

Supporting Family Caregivers

Millions of adult children and spouses care for older adults without guidance. We provide respectful, dignity-first communication tools and safety sheets to support their caregiving journey.

🩺 Healthcare Advocacy

Bridging the Clinical Gap

During a standard 15-minute doctor visit, physicians rarely have enough time to instruct patients on proper balance exercises, safe chair-to-stand mechanics, or room-by-room home hazard mitigation.

Evergreen Aging functions as an educational bridge between clinical guidance and everyday home life. We translate medical longevity literature into printable daily routines, home safety checklists, and dignity-first habits seniors and families can adopt directly at home.

🛡️

Our Clinical Sourcing & Verification Policy

To protect senior health and counter health misinformation on the internet, Evergreen Aging enforces a strict Zero-Speculation Sourcing Standard. We do not promote unverified supplements, fad diets, or non-peer-reviewed wellness trends.

🇨🇦

Health Canada & PHAC

Canadian Physical Activity Guidelines for Seniors 65+, Seniors' Falls in Canada epidemiology, and Canada's Dietary Guidelines for aging adults.

🏥

Mayo Clinic Health System

Clinical guidelines on joint preservation in osteoarthritis, progressive strength for sarcopenia, senior hydration physiology, and ambulatory balance therapy.

🛡️

CDC STEADI Initiative

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention's Stopping Elderly Accidents, Deaths, & Injuries clinical algorithm for primary care.

🔬

National Institute on Aging (NIH)

Evidence-based longevity science regarding Alzheimer's/dementia risk factors, sleep architecture, and caregiver resilience.

Transparent Verification: Every video lesson and printable toolkit on this site displays its clinical citation, authorizing body, and a direct link to the original government or hospital publication.
